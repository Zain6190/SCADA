01 · The problem

The Indus runs on lead time

A flood wave, an irrigation release, and a dam's inflow share one river — but operators read them from separate, lagged sources.

Flood

The 2022 floods affected ~33 million people across Pakistan.

Warnings exist. Coordination between bulletin, gauge, and ground truth does not.

Irrigation

The Indus is the world's largest contiguous irrigation network — every release is a decision with a downstream price.

Canal schedules run on historical habits, not on this week's rainfall or snowmelt.

Operations

Bulletins, spreadsheets, phone calls — no single pane of glass, no audit trail, no ownership when an alert fires at 02:00.

Sensors report; nobody is assigned.

Our answer Ingest → validate → forecast → alert → assign the work, in one audited system.

03 · Data truth · Sentinel-2 surface water

One methodology, every week

What was wrong

  • Mean reducer over water mask → ~0 km² everywhere
  • No cloud budget, no date filter — scenes from any season
  • Partial windows overwrote full history in the CSV
  • Demo rows seeded with a different methodology → fake Δ%

What ships now

  • Single-pass NDWI+MNDWI composite, service-account GEE auth
  • Sum-based water area × 30 m pixels — area is area
  • Cloud-aware week window + tileScale ladder (60→100)
  • Merge-on-write CSV — partial runs can't destroy history
  • Demo rows deleted; every Δ shares one methodology

05 · Machine learning · forecasting

Forecast with intervals, not confidence theatre

Flood predictor — 7 / 14 / 30 day

  • Quantile forecasts q10–q90 → 80% interval with measured coverage
  • Walk-forward R² is the primary metric — a single split lies under drift
  • Beats persistence baseline or it does not ship
  • Horizon with no registry row → badge UNREGISTERED, not a fake score

Organic accuracy loop

  • Every matured prediction lands in prediction_errors
  • accuracy = 1 − MAPE on real data, per horizon
  • Below min-sample → “Not validated” instead of a number
  • Degradation feeds UC-6 model-quality alerts

Scored like a forecast, not a demo

  • Walk-forward AUC + Brier skill vs baseline + precision/recall
  • Score ≥ 70 → SHADOW, ≥ 40 → EXPERIMENTAL, else REJECTED
  • No positive or no negative labels → refuses to score

One hard serving gate

  • A REJECTED classifier never reaches forecasts, maps, or alerts
  • Gate reads the registry on every tick — rollback is one click

08 · Workflow

Alerts are events — work is assigned

NEW─ack─▶ ACKNOWLEDGED─work─▶ INVESTIGATING─report·verify─▶ RESOLVED
SLA 15/30 min ──▶ ESCALATED INFO only ──▶ AUTO_RESOLVED Supervisor issues instruction ──▶ Officer reports ──▶ Verified

The loop

  • 5 roles: admin · supervisor · officer · analyst · viewer — every action writes actor + role to an append-only event log
  • Instructions carry due times; missed due → auto-escalate
  • Alert can't close while its instructions are open
  • UC-1…UC-8: forecast escalation, threshold breach, outage, accuracy drop, water stress, episode rollup
