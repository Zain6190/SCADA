DAILY FLOOD BULLETIN 26-AUG-2026

Issued: 26 Aug 2026, 11:30  ·  Published: 26 Aug 2026, 11:55
Permalink Download PDF
GOVERNMENT OF PAKISTAN
MINISTRY OF DEFENCE (DEFENCE DIVISION)
PAKISTAN METEOROLOGICAL DEPARTMENT
FLOOD FORECASTING DIVISION
46-JAIL ROAD LAHORE-54000
BULLETIN No: 073/26 Dated: 26th August-2026 Time: 11:30 hours (PST)

RIVERS AT A GLANCE

FLOOD SEVERITY SCALE

RAINFALL FORECAST — NEXT 24 & 48 HOURS

RIVERS SITUATION AND QUALITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST

I. METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES 

  • A fresh trough of westerly wave lies over northwest of Iran.

  • Seasonal low-pressure area lies over northeast Balochistan.

  • Yesterday's low pressure system over south Jharkhand has moved westwards and today lies over northeast Madhya Pradesh (India). 

  • Weak moist currents from Arabian sea are penetrating into the upper parts of the country up to 3,000 feet.

II. METEOROLOGICAL OUTLOOK              

  • Mainly hot and humid weather is expected over most parts of the country, however, Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of Sutlej and Ravi rivers.

 

III. HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at 0600 PST

  • Tarbela reservoir is currently at its Maximum Conservation Level, while Mangla reservoir is 75% filled.  

  • River Indus at Chashma, Taunsa, Guddu and Sukkur is at Low Flood Level (downstream).

     

IV. HYDROLOGICAL OUTLOOK

  • River Indus at Guddu  is likely to remain Medium Flood Level (upstream) within next 24-hours.

V. WEATHER FORECAST FOR 24-HOURS

  • Mainly hot and humid weather is expected over most parts of the country with increasing trend in temperatures, however, Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of Sutlej and Ravi rivers.

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Batagram, Khyber, Kohistan, Mansehra, Orakzai, Shangla, Swat and Dir.

VI. WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT 48-HOURS   

  • Mainly hot and humid weather is expected over most parts of the country, however, Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of Sutlej, Ravi and Chenab rivers, Islamabad, Kashmir,

    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Abbottabad, Batagram, Dir, Kohistan, Mansehra, Shangla and Swat. 

    Punjab: Gujrat, Narowal, Rawalpindi and Sialkot. 

WEEKLY SIGNIFICANT OUTLOOK 

(27th August, 2026 to 02nd  September, 2026)

I. RAINFALL OUTLOOK

  • A monsoon trough is expected to affect upper catchments of all the major rivers moderately from 28th August to 02nd September. Consequently, scattered to widespread rainfall activity is expected in the upper catchments of all the major rivers during the period.

II. FLOOD OUTLOOK

  • On account of expected monsoon trough from 28th August to 02nd September, flows in rivers Jhelum, Chenab and Ravi along with their associated nullahs are expected to increase moderately during the period.

4. QUANTITATIVE FLOOD FORECAST OF GAUGING STATIONS (IN THOUSANDS OF CUSECS)

Note: Concerned Departments may contact FFD, Lahore for rectification up-dation (if any)

5. HOW CLOSE IS EACH RIVER TO ITS LIMIT?

Guddu is in low to medium flood at 351.6 — 148.4 below its high flood level (500.0). Each bar is one station’s own flood levels across its maximum capacity; the solid run is the flow so far, the pale run is the room still left.

All figures in thousands of cusecs. Full bar = maximum (design) capacity. Solid colour is the flow reached so far and the pale wash beside it is the room still left; the dark line between them is the current reading, printed beneath it. Thin white lines are that station’s flood levels with their thresholds beneath, so the same colour can begin at a different point on every bar. Each band is named inside itself — initialled (BL, L, M, H, VH, EH) where the band is too narrow for the words, and left blank where it is too narrow for those. Stations without a design capacity are not shown.
